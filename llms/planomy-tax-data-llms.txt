# Planomy

> Planomy is a free, local-first US personal-finance and retirement planner. Its
> browser-based tools model year-by-year cash flow, federal and state tax, Social
> Security, Medicare and IRMAA, required minimum distributions, Roth conversions,
> Monte Carlo outcomes and historical returns without requiring an account.

Planomy is educational planning software, not financial, tax or investment advice.
The core planner and every calculator are free; plan data stays on the user's device
unless the user chooses an account and cloud sync.
